Forecast. Seal. Score.

A forecast only means something if it was fixed before the answer existed. So the answer is the one thing Brier Lab never gets to see first.

01

The model reads the trial

It studies the public registration record: the drug, the design, the endpoint, the sponsor. Then it gives one probability.

02

The forecast is sealed

Every forecast is hashed into a single fingerprint. Change one number afterward and the fingerprint no longer matches.

03

Reality grades it

When the sponsor reports, the forecast is scored against the result. Hits and misses stay on the record.

    How it is scored

    By Brier score, the standard measure for probability forecasts, shown next to a plain statistical baseline. Lower is better; zero is perfect.

    Brier

    How a result is decided

    Each result is recorded with a link to its source and the words that decide it. A trial stopped for futility, lack of efficacy or safety counts as not met. A trial stopped for any other reason is void and is not scored. Results already public when we sealed are not scored. Each forecaster is scored on its last forecast sealed at least a day before the result was reported.
