01 / THE RECORD

It starts with the public record.

Every drug trial is registered before it runs: the drug, the design, the endpoint, the sponsor. That record is where every forecast begins.

02 / THE READ

The model reads the trial.

It weighs how often trials like this succeed, how hard the endpoint is to hit, and whether the trial is big enough to show an effect. Out comes one number.

03 / THE EVIDENCE

Then it checks the evidence.

For trials close to reporting, Brier Lab gathers sourced evidence on the drug and its class. The forecast moves only as far as the evidence justifies.

04 / THE SEAL

The forecast is sealed.

Each forecast is hashed. The hashes fold together, pair by pair, into a single fingerprint for the whole record.

05 / THE WAIT

Nothing can change while we wait.

Alter one number and the fingerprint stops matching. The forecast you see after the result is the forecast that was made before it.

06 / THE SCORE

Reality grades it.

When the sponsor reports, the forecast is scored. A confident call that turns out wrong costs the most, and every score stays on the record.
